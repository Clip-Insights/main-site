Clip Insights uses the video's transcript to support AI summaries, key points and questions. The system does not watch a diagram in the way you do. A detail shown only on screen may be missing from a transcript-based answer.

## What each tool does

- **Summary** gives an overview of the transcript's main ideas.
- **Key Points** draws out concise takeaways from the available text.
- **Chat** lets you ask questions about the video using transcript context.
- **Transcript** copies available transcript text for your own review.

Sign-in and account quotas apply to AI actions. Account limits are available through the [web app's plans page](https://app.clipinsights.org/pricing). Longer transcripts may be limited by the amount of text processed per request, so a response should not be treated as a guarantee of complete video coverage.

## Start with the original explanation

Watch the relevant section first. Then ask a question that identifies a concept: “Why does the speaker copy the array before sorting it?” is easier to evaluate than “Explain this whole course.”

Ask a follow-up about the missing link. If an answer introduces a claim you cannot locate in the video, verify it independently rather than copying it into your notes as a fact.

## When results are incomplete

Some videos have no usable captions. Captions can also misrecognize names, formulas, punctuation or code. An answer based on incorrect source text can be fluent and still wrong.

If a feature reports that the transcript is unavailable, try another video with captions to distinguish a video-specific issue from a wider service issue. Reload once if the page has just navigated to a different video. Repeatedly requesting AI output will not create a transcript that does not exist.

## What is sent for processing

When you request AI output, transcript content and relevant video information are sent to the backend. Chat also includes your question and relevant conversation context. The backend uses service providers to generate answers and, where configured, retrieve relevant transcript passages. See [Privacy](/privacy) before including sensitive information in a question.

## Check before you keep

Use a simple three-part check: can you find the idea in the original video, does the transcript accurately reflect it, and can you explain the answer in your own words? Preserve an unresolved question as a note when you are unsure.

AI features depend on backend availability. A working website or extension panel does not mean every remote service is available. Local notes and PDF downloads do not need an AI response.
