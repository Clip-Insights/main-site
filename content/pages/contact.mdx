The email link opens your own mail application. If you use browser-based email, copy the address and compose a message there. This page does not submit or store a contact form.

## For a technical problem

Include the action you attempted, the error you saw, your Chrome version and your extension version. A public YouTube link can help us reproduce a video-specific issue. Before sharing a screenshot, hide private notes and personal information.

Never send your password, Google sign-in tokens, API keys or payment-card details. We do not need them to investigate a bug. You can also check the [troubleshooting guide](/guides/troubleshooting) first.

## For account or privacy requests

Send your request from the email address associated with your Clip Insights account where possible. Describe whether you want access, correction, or deletion of account data and uploaded files. We may need to confirm that you control the account before acting on a request.

Local extension notes are stored in your browser. Contacting us does not give us access to that local collection. Export a PDF before removing local data you want to keep.

## For feedback

Tell us what you were studying, what you expected and where you became stuck. Clip Insights is a small project; we do not promise a fixed support response time, but specific reports help us address the right problem.
