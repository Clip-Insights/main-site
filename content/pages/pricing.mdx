## Without an account

You can take timestamped notes, capture video frames and download your study material as a PDF in the extension, within guest limits. These tools store their working data in your browser. Keep an exported copy before clearing browser data or removing the extension.

## With a free account

Sign in to use AI features and upload PDFs to your web library, within the allowances for your account. AI actions depend on transcript availability and the backend being available. Cloud storage and individual files also have limits.

## Check the current allowances

The [web app's plans page](https://app.clipinsights.org/pricing) reads plan values from the backend. Use it to check current note, screenshot, AI and storage allowances. We keep one plan catalogue rather than duplicating numbers here that could become outdated.

If the plan page cannot load, try again when the service is available or [contact us](/contact). This marketing page does not display cached prices as though they were live offers.

## Paid plans

Paid checkout is not currently available. Higher tiers may appear as previews in the app, but there is no paid subscription to purchase through this public site. No card is required to install the extension or start using guest tools.

[Install the Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ccgechmifoecebnimnccgahnoklklilj) or [read the getting-started guide](/guides/getting-started).
