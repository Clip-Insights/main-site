## Start with the essentials

Install Clip Insights and turn your next YouTube lesson into study material. Take timestamped notes, capture useful frames and download a PDF to revisit later. You can get started as a guest, with no card required.

## Bring more to your study session

A free account gives you access to transcript-based summaries, key points and video chat within your account allowances. Ask questions as you learn, then upload your exported PDFs to a personal web library.

Your notes stay beside the video while you work. Your library gives your finished study documents a place to return to.

## Find the right fit

Visit [Plans in the web app](https://app.clipinsights.org/pricing) to see the current allowances for notes, screenshots, AI and storage. You can review your options there as your study routine grows.

[Add Clip Insights to Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ccgechmifoecebnimnccgahnoklklilj) and [start your first study session](/guides/getting-started).
