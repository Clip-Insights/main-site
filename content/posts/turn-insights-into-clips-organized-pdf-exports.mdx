An exported study document should explain why you kept a moment, not simply prove that you watched a video. Clip Insights combines saved notes and screenshots into a PDF you can keep outside the browser.

This article was first published in 2024 and has been revised to clarify current export and storage behavior.

## Prepare the material

Capture a meaningful frame, add an explanation in your own words and keep its timestamp. Before export, remove accidental duplicates and make unresolved questions recognizable as questions.

A useful note might explain why a code change fixes a problem. A screenshot of the final code alone may not preserve that reasoning.

## Download and check

Select **Download** in the extension. Open the generated PDF and inspect its notes and images. Keep it with the course or project where you will need it, using a filename that identifies the topic.

A PDF is a snapshot of your saved material at export time. Later edits in the extension do not update an earlier downloaded document. Export again when you need a revised copy.

## Upload is a separate choice

When signed in, select **Upload** to send a generated PDF to your account. File-size and account-storage limits apply. You can manage uploaded files in the [web app](https://app.clipinsights.org).

Local notes do not automatically synchronize. Keep a downloaded copy before clearing browser data or removing the extension, even if you have used the web app before.

## Share with context

Preserve the source video information. Respect the creator's rights and course policies before distributing frames or quotations, and check the document for private information.
