# Combined public-site plan

## Reuse the existing foundation

Continue the Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind and Velite project in this repository. Preserve the WordPress migration helpers, original article slugs and dates, taxonomy routes, redirect map and content-query tests. This replaces the earlier proposal to start an Astro project, written before the existing repository was discovered.

## Complete the visitor journey

Lead with the YouTube use case: capture a frame, attach an explanation and timestamp, explore a transcript where useful, and export a PDF. The primary action is extension installation; returning users can open the existing app. Product illustrations use original demonstration content and are labelled accordingly.

Retain the existing logo and indigo palette. White surfaces, a clear type hierarchy and substantial product scenes explain the tool. The hero's interactive walkthrough switches between Notes, Insights and PDF. It does not pretend to call a live AI service.

## Content included

- Product homepage and detailed features.
- Four complete guides: getting started, notes/PDFs, AI/transcripts and troubleshooting.
- Six new practical articles plus five revised WordPress imports.
- Blog pagination, author/tag archives, reading time, article contents links and related reading.
- About, contact, plans/access, privacy and terms pages.
- A genuine sample generated by the extension's PDF renderer from original illustration material.
- Missing-page handling, sitemap, robots, RSS, social image and local fonts.

The confirmed contact address is zubayrahmad0308@gmail.com. Contact uses a real email link and a copy control, without a dummy form. Pricing points to the app's current catalogue and accurately states that checkout is not available. Local notes and uploaded PDFs are described separately.

## Research that informed the design

- [Eightify](https://eightify.app/): specific YouTube purpose and concrete examples.
- [Glasp](https://glasp.co/): capture-to-revisit narrative and clear installation path.
- [Readwise Reader](https://readwise.io/read): product scenes paired with recognizable tasks.
- [Raindrop](https://raindrop.io/): organization benefits and clear app/download destinations.
- [Notion Web Clipper](https://www.notion.com/web-clipper): concise first-screen explanation.
- [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content): useful original editorial content.
- [Chrome privacy requirements](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/privacy): accurate data-use disclosures and a public policy link.

These are structural references, not proof of conversion performance. No competitor screenshots, testimonials, logos or claims are copied.
